Digital infrastructure has collapsed.

Yet the cloud did not disappear.

Data migrated to the vegetal network.

Flowers store it.

Pollen carries it.

Bees transmit it.

You are a bee within this network.

Travel between flowers,

and collect data-pollens.

130 petabytes will give you a password,

and unlock a secret message.

Controls

Look around with your mouse for data-pollen.

Use the space bar to fly in that direction.

Press F to collect it when you are close enough.

To exit the game or reveal the secret message, press the Escape key twice on your keyboard.
